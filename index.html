📖 Fake News Survival Toolkit

Essential cognitive frameworks used by professional fact-checkers to debunk disinformation before it spreads.

🧭

The S.I.F.T. Method

Stop. Investigate the source. Find better coverage. Trace claims back to the original source.

🧠

Emotional Trigger Radar

If an article triggers sudden outrage or terror, pause. Disinformation is intentionally engineered to bypass analytical reasoning.

🖼️

Reverse Image Inspection

Recycled visuals are common. Always reverse-search images using Google Lens or TinEye to check timestamps and context.

🔎

URL Domain Typosquatting

Inspect top-level domains. Fake news outlets mimic legitimate agencies using lookalike domains (e.g. .co, .news-today.xyz).
